The return moment

/coming-back is the screen somebody meets after weeks away, and it had not been looked at since phase 8. Redesign ticket 35 redraws it as a step under DIRECTION.md rule 15 and gives it the five movements it owes. Everything here is trans, light, at 390×844.

Everything below is only this screen. Every number is read off the built app at 390×844 with reduce-motion off, and the “before” column is main at b9e88296, recorded the same way.

What the rule asked for

Rule 15 names this route in as many words: a step is one purpose, no navigation and a short list, and it takes the field with a title, the flush list, and rule 12’s foot with one control. Three things followed from that.

  • No tab bar and no back chevron. The route is chromeless now, the way the in-the-room view is: a bar floating over a moment is four more ways off it, and the foot already carries the one. A step has no parent, and a moment linked from nowhere has nothing above it to go back to.
  • One flush list, not two surfaces. Rule 12 forbids a notice on a step. What the phase 8 review wanted from two cards was that the ordering be visible — a letter from somebody’s past self is not a peer of a binder timer — and role 0 with a chevron against role 1 with two labelled controls carries that without a container.
  • The offer row keeps its labelled yes. That was the other half of the same review. The icon block, the title and the reason line are rule 13’s row exactly as it draws one; what rule 13 does not spell out is a row whose answer is two controls rather than a tick or a switch, and this ticket adds that case. The yes is --text and the no is --text-2, both underlined at 2px like every other text action in phase 10, both at the full 48px target, 20px apart. Nearest reference: Cash App’s notification screen, which the fourth sweep read for the permissions step — a title, a reason and two buttons, flush on the page. Its icon is not where this block comes from; that is rule 6’s row icon.

The five movements

Chromium’s own screencast off the built app, one frame per paint, with the millisecond it landed. The readout beside each reel is a separate per-animation-frame sample of what that movement animates, taken in the same scene: a recording shows that something moved, and the numbers say by how much and when. Drag the scrubber, or use the arrow keys.

The same five, clamped

With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce set. Substitute, never delete: a clip becomes a cut, a height travel becomes a removal, the sheet fades where it stands.

Before, for the two that are comparable

The arrival and an answered row, recorded off main in the same scenes. The row is the one worth naming: the screen’s own comment on main says the notice “leaves through its own disclose so answering collapses the space it held”, and the built app did not do that.

What the ticket did not change

  • Every one of ADR-0062’s decisions. Three weeks, once per gap, linked from nowhere, no count of what was missed, no notification, backfill per item only.
  • The reads behind the screen, and whether more than one dose slot is ever offered.
  • No figure anywhere states how much was missed, and a backfill still leaves the record’s holes where they were: the dose sheet says so in as many words.
  • Each offer is answerable once and mints nothing without confirmation.

One copy change the redraw forced. The line under the field is rule 12’s one line, two at most, and the old intro set three at 15px; the half worth keeping is the permission, so it reads “Take what you want, leave the rest.” and the title carries the rest. The two sheets’ second control says Cancel now rather than the offer’s own Leave it: the row behind the sheet wears those words and they answer the offer, and closing a sheet does not.
