First stable release of engender. Entries, photos and care records stay on your device. Works offline, in English and Polish. Updating from 1.0.0-rc.1 removes measurements, affirmations, the journaling pause, saved questions and the hormone curve: export an encrypted archive first. Encrypted archives move your journal between web and Android. Development journals are not supported. Play uses its own signing key, so moving to or from Play means reinstalling and restoring an archive.
