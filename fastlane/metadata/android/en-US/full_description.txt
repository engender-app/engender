Keep a record of your transition you can return to. Write down how a new name feels or what's changed since starting HRT, with photos and care records alongside your notes.

engender is made by trans people for other trans people. Use whichever parts fit your life, whether you're questioning, planning your next step or years into transition. It's free, no ads, subscriptions or accounts to create.

Room for how you feel

A mood is enough for an entry. When you have more to say, add a note, photo or voice recording. Or record how your gender feels using gender scales. You can write several times a day and use tags to find things later. Give dysphoria, euphoria and other feelings their own scales, with names and endpoints you choose. You can also record how you feel about different parts of your body.

Keep your care records together

Track HRT and other medication with dose logs, schedules and reminders. Keep lab results alongside body measurements and changes you notice, including hair changes and cycle tracking if you use it. For surgery, keep your plans and recovery notes together. Before an appointment, gather your questions and prepare a clinician summary to take with you.

engender does not diagnose conditions, interpret lab results, recommend doses or replace medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about treatment decisions.

The things you want to try and remember

Trying a name, different pronouns or a new look? Note how it feels over time. Record voice practice and compare it with your earlier recordings. Keep track of hair removal and time spent binding or tucking. Mark your milestones, plan your next steps, or write yourself a letter to open on a date you choose.

See what has changed

Put photos side by side, follow your scales on charts or revisit a week, month or year. Browse your calendar whenever you want; days without entries stay empty. Search your notes and tags when you want to find a particular moment. Safe Space keeps favourite entries and your own list of things that help within reach on harder days.

Your journal stays with you

The Android app works offline and has no internet permission. It sends no journal data or usage statistics to a server. You decide when to export or share anything.

Your stored journal and media are encrypted. Some startup settings and key-management metadata remain outside that encryption, and taking a photo can leave a temporary unencrypted camera file before import. The privacy policy explains these limits.

You can unlock with a passphrase, PIN or your phone's biometric or screen lock. Optional disguise mode gives the app a neutral name and icon.

Keep a copy, take it with you

Save a password-encrypted backup or schedule backups to a folder you choose. An archive lets you move your journal to another device or the web app. You can bring in entries from Daylio CSV, too.

CSV, JSON, printable journal books and other readable exports are not encrypted. Anyone with those files can read them. Keep a backup off your device if you want to be able to restore your journal after losing it.
