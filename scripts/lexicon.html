Lexicon

One name per thing, in both languages. A term lists the English forms, the Polish forms in every case and number, and the stems of variants to avoid. Changing a form rewrites every string that uses it, form by form: schemat to plan also turns schematu into planu. You see every change before it is saved.

Two kinds of finding. Polish does not use the term: the English has the term but the Polish has none of its forms, so the Polish names the thing some other way, or the English word is used in another sense. Avoided word: the string uses a word listed under Avoid. Fix the string in the finding, or mark it as not a problem. A dismissed finding comes back if the string changes.
