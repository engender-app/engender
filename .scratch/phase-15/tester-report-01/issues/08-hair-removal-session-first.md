## What to build

Open Hair removal on the person's recent sessions instead of a long list of body regions. Keep region tracking on the same screen, with a control that collapses and reopens the list. Recording a session and finding an existing region must remain straightforward.

- [ ] Check long region names, 390px width, 200% zoom and reduced motion. Existing session data and archive behavior do not change.
